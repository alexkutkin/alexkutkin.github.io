---
lang: en
title: Readability over brevity
description: A short line saves a minute; a clear line saves hours at the next change.
date: 2026-10-05
translationKey: readability-over-brevity
---

Clever code is a loan against the next reader. It feels efficient while it is being written and costs interest every time someone has to change it.

I would rather read three obvious lines than one dense expression with two nested ternaries. The compiler does not care, and the next person will.

The same rule applies to names. A slightly longer name that says what the value is beats a short one that needs a comment. Comments explain why; names explain what.
