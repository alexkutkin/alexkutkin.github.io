title: Grid before mockup
description: Axes and rhythm come first; decorative decisions come after.
date: 2026-09-27
translationKey: grid-before-mockup
---

A mockup shows one screen at one width. A grid explains how that screen behaves at every other width.

I start with the reading column, the page margins and the steps between blocks. Those choices decide where content can go long before a border radius is discussed.

Decoration is easier to add than structure is to remove. If the rhythm is right, the page still works when the decoration is stripped away.
