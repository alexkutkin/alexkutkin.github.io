---
lang: en
title: Slow checks
description: Automation is useful as long as its result can be explained and reproduced.
date: 2026-09-23
translationKey: slow-checks
---

A check that nobody understands is a ritual. It turns green and everyone moves on, until it turns red and nobody knows why.

A useful check has a clear failure message, a fixed starting point and a result that can be reproduced outside the pipeline. It may be slower than a clever one-liner, and that is fine.

The goal is not to run more checks. The goal is to trust the ones we run.
