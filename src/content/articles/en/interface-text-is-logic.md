title: Interface text is logic
description: The wording of a button sets expectations no less than its handler.
date: 2026-10-01
translationKey: interface-text-is-logic
---

A button labelled “Continue” promises something different from one labelled “Pay”. The handler may be identical; the user's understanding is not.

Interface text is often treated as a final polish, but it encodes decisions. “Delete” needs a confirmation step because the word itself describes a consequence. “Hide” does not.

When a flow feels confusing, I read the labels without looking at the screens. If they do not tell a coherent story, no amount of layout will fix it.
